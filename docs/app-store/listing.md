## 繁體中文（主要語言）

**名稱**
平安行車通

**副標題**
OBD 行車電腦・即時儀表板

**宣傳文字**
橫向全螢幕行車儀表板：車速、轉速雙環即時顯示，水溫、油耗、電瓶電壓隨你釘選；超速語音提醒、懸浮車速視窗、故障碼查詢。搭配藍牙 OBD-II 轉接器使用。

**關鍵字**
OBD,OBD2,行車電腦,儀表板,車速,轉速,水溫,油耗,故障碼,診斷,藍牙,汽車,速度警示,G值,水平儀

**描述**
把手機變成車上的即時儀表板。

「平安行車通」連接藍牙 OBD-II 轉接器，讀取車輛的即時資料，用橫向全螢幕的簡潔介面呈現。一眼看懂車況，不必低頭翻選單。

【主要功能】
● 雙環儀表：外環車速、內環轉速，並以 5 秒峰值標記顯示剛剛的最高值
● 自訂首頁：最多釘選 8 個參數卡片（水溫、電瓶電壓、引擎負載、油耗…），長按即可拖曳排序、左右移動或移除
● 行車電腦：瞬時／平均油耗（依進氣流量估算）、行駛里程、行駛時間
● 更多即時參數：依引擎、溫度、壓力、燃油排放、電力等分頁，左右滑動瀏覽
● 超速語音提醒：車速到達 110、120、130 km/h 時以語音報讀
● 懸浮車速視窗：切換到地圖或導航 App 時，車速仍以子母畫面浮在上方
● 故障碼讀取：常見故障碼附中文說明，其餘顯示英文原文
● 手機感測器輔助：OBD 訊號中斷時改用 GPS 車速備援；另顯示行進方向、海拔、G 值與車身左右傾角
● 自動辨識車廠：依車身號碼（VIN）載入對應的廠牌增強參數（部分車款）
● 自動重連：藍牙短暫中斷後自動重新連線

【使用前請注意】
‧ 需要另購支援藍牙低功耗（BLE）的 OBD-II 轉接器（例如 vLinker 系列），並插在車輛的 OBD-II 診斷座。本 App 與轉接器廠商無隸屬關係。
‧ 能讀到哪些參數取決於車輛與年份；車輛不支援的參數會顯示「--」。
‧ 油耗、G 值、傾角等為依感測器資料估算，僅供參考，不等同原廠儀表。
‧ 行車中請專心駕駛，設定請於停車時進行，並遵守當地交通法規。

【隱私】
本 App 不蒐集、不上傳任何個人資料，所有資料只在你的手機上處理。定位僅在使用 App 期間用於 GPS 車速、方向與海拔顯示。

## English (U.S.)

**Name**
平安行車通

**Subtitle**
OBD trip computer & dashboard

**Promotional Text**
Landscape driving dashboard: live speed and RPM rings, pinnable gauges, spoken speed alerts, floating speed window, trouble codes. Needs a Bluetooth OBD-II adapter.

**Keywords**
OBD,OBD2,dashboard,speedometer,tachometer,trip computer,fuel economy,DTC,car diagnostics,G-force

**Description**
Turn your iPhone into a live in-car dashboard.

平安行車通 connects to a Bluetooth OBD-II adapter, reads live data from your vehicle and shows it in a clean landscape full-screen layout, so you can read your car's status at a glance.

KEY FEATURES
• Dual-ring gauge: speed on the outer ring, RPM on the inner ring, each with a 5-second peak-hold marker
• Customizable home screen: pin up to 8 gauge cards (coolant temperature, battery voltage, engine load, fuel economy and more); long-press to reorder, move between sides or remove
• Trip computer: instant and average fuel economy (estimated from mass air flow), trip distance and trip time
• More live readings: swipe through pages for engine, temperature, pressure, fuel & emissions and electrical data
• Spoken speed alerts when you reach 110, 120 and 130 km/h
• Floating speed window (Picture in Picture) that stays on top of your maps or navigation app
• Read trouble codes (common codes have Traditional Chinese explanations; others show the English original)
• Phone sensors as a helper: GPS speed fallback if the OBD signal drops, plus heading, altitude, G-force and vehicle roll angle
• Automatic make detection from the VIN to load make-specific extended readings (selected vehicles)
• Automatic reconnect after a brief Bluetooth drop

BEFORE YOU START
• A Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) OBD-II adapter (for example the vLinker series) is sold separately and plugs into your vehicle's OBD-II port. This app is not affiliated with any adapter manufacturer.
• Which readings are available depends on the vehicle and model year; unsupported readings show "--".
• Fuel economy, G-force and roll angle are estimates derived from sensor data and are for reference only; they are not the factory instrument values.
• Keep your eyes on the road. Change settings only while parked and follow local traffic laws.

PRIVACY
The app collects no personal data and uploads nothing; all data is processed on your device. Location is used only while the app is in use, for GPS speed, heading and altitude.

This app is a dashboard for Bluetooth LE OBD-II adapters (for example the vLinker series). Its main readings (RPM, coolant temperature, fuel economy, trouble codes...) come from a physical adapter plugged into a car's OBD-II port with the ignition on, so they cannot be shown on a review device without that hardware. A short demo video of the app connected to a real vehicle is linked below.

Background modes:
- bluetooth-central: keep the adapter connection alive while the screen locks or the user switches to a maps app.
